## Who we are

CNCMS is provided by OneTech, Kumba, Cameroon. You can reach us at onetechcncms@gmail.com or +237 672 528 022.

## What we keep

- **About your company:** its name, contact details and settings.
- **About your staff:** names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, roles and what they do in CNCMS.
- **About your customers:** what your staff enter, such as names, phone numbers, zones, services, bills and payments.
- **Technical records:** sign-in times, IP addresses and an activity log of actions in the system.

## Why we keep it

Only to run CNCMS for your company: to show your data to your staff, keep your accounts correct, protect your account, and help you when you contact us.

## Who can see it

- Your company's data is seen only by your own staff, each according to the role you give them.
- OneTech support staff may look at it only to help you or to fix a problem, and this is recorded.
- We never sell your data, and we never share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

## How we protect it

Passwords are stored in a form nobody can read. Managers can turn on two-step login. Every action is recorded. Encrypted backups are made every night and kept in two separate secure places.

## How long we keep it

As long as your company uses CNCMS. If you stop, we keep your data so you can come back or export it, until you ask us in writing to delete it.

## Your choices

You can see, correct and export your company's data at any time. To ask us to delete it, or for any question about your data, write to onetechcncms@gmail.com.

## Changes

When we change this policy, we publish the new version here with its date. If the change is important, we tell your company's administrators in CNCMS.
